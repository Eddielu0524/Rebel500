Collected 2026-09-30 for the local 3D reconstruction. All photos remain Honda's copyrighted reference material; these are reference photos, not an official Honda CAD model. ## Material hierarchy inferred from photographs

Use distinct finishes rather than one universal black. Painted tank/fenders have broad smooth clear-coat reflections; tubular frame is black coated metal; cast engine cases are charcoal with fine roughness; radiator plastic and airbox panels are less reflective; tires are near-black rough rubber; seat is black vinyl with fine grain, soft highlight and stitched perimeter. Exposed silver is limited to selected fasteners, fork tubes, cap, cam plugs, brake discs and exhaust cap. Avoid silver wheel spoke faces: the observed rims/spokes are black with specular edge reflections.

## Limits

Photographs cannot reveal hidden geometry or certify accessory mount compatibility. Some official studio imagery may itself be CG, as Honda Japan notes. Reconstruction should be described as photo-referenced rather than a factory CAD or scan. Preserve the actual interactive 3D geometry and accessory controls; photo references supplement the model and are not a substitute for it.

## Material reference review - 2026-10-03

This pass compared the local Honda tank, engine, wheel and headlight closeups with accessible official pages and the user sources described below. Visual observations are rendering guidance, not measurements of paint chemistry, roughness, or reflectance.

### Source checks and trim differences

- Honda Taiwan product page (live): https://moto.honda-taiwan.com.tw/motor/Detail/681dd145-be45-4248-a34e-8c554ccc8e8b — explicitly describes the Taiwan 2025 S equipment as headlight cowl, fork covers/gaiters, **black** diamond-pattern saddle and bronze wheels. Brown rider and passenger saddles appear separately as optional accessories. Keep this market distinction when offering a brown-seat configuration.
- Honda Europe 2025 announcement: https://hondanews.eu/gb/en/motorcycles/media/pressreleases/502901/improved-ergonomics-updated-technology-and-another-new-member-ensure-hondas-cmx-family-goes-from-str — the search-indexed official text describes the European S with a smoke-screen headlight cowl, brown diamond-pattern saddle and bronze wheels. It is a regional styling reference rather than evidence that the Taiwan standard model has those parts.
- Honda UK current CMX500 page: https://www.honda.co.uk/motorcycles/range/street/cmx500-rebel/overview.html — lists Matte Gunpowder Black Metallic for the shown European model. Do not apply the European matte finish to the glossy Taiwan/Japan black reference without an explicit paint choice.

### Direct observations from local official closeups

- `honda-detail-13.png`: the black tank reflects recognizable surrounding shapes. Its finish is smooth gloss with a dark body color; the broad highlight is an environment reflection, not a light-gray base paint. Small grain should not break the smooth silhouette or turn it into visibly hammered metal.
- `honda-tw-tank.jpg` and `honda-tw-engine.jpg`: black frame tubes have narrow, strong reflection strips. Engine castings instead show fine charcoal grain and broad weak reflections; the large circular cover is smoother than the upper casting. Painted black tubes, cast cases and radiator/airbox plastic must remain separate finishes. Photographs alone do not establish whether each coating is powder coat.
- `honda-detail-09.png`: tire sidewalls carry broad muted highlights and subdued raised molding; tire tread grooves are crisp but not deep off-road blocks. Black cast spokes reflect more sharply than rubber, while the brake rotor is bright brushed metal. The black wheel faces should not be simulated as exposed silver.
- `honda-2025-black-001.jpg`: the stock saddle is a dark, slightly satin surface with a soft edge highlight; the tank and fenders are more reflective. The muffler is satin black with a restrained broad highlight and a contrasting silver end cap.
- `honda-tw-headlight.jpg`: the S-style cowl shell has a fine satin dark finish while its short upper smoked insert has a stronger, smoother reflection. The insert should read as tinted transparent material rather than milky frosted plastic.

### Supplemental accessory material guidance

- Honda 2025 accessories catalog: https://www.honda-mc.dk/media/hsoaln0y/book_accessories_2025_web_02jan_16h.pdf — official catalog search text identifies brown/black diamond-pattern seats. Another seat entry describes a welded surface pattern, but that process is not verified for the CMX500 seat; it is only generic visual guidance. Thin recessed seam channels and subtle padded diamonds are the artistic target, not a claim about this seat’s manufacturing process. The catalog covers multiple models; part numbers must not be copied to this project's 2025 CMX500 fitment without separate verification.
- Puig manufacturer example for Rebel 500 2025: https://puigusa.com/en/tuning-motos/carenabrises-universales-para-faros-no-redondos/windshield-upampdown?bike=37702 — lists a 3 mm high-impact acrylic screen in Smoke and Clear. This establishes plausible accessory material, not that the project's generic screen is this exact product.
- Puig manufacturer touring example for Rebel 500 2024: https://puigusa.com/en/tuning-motos/windshields-specific-for-non-round-headlights/new-generation-touring?bike=35401 — describes PMMA and distinct smoke, dark-smoke, clear and opaque-black versions. For a smoked transparent visual, preserve a legible background through the pane, darken transmitted light and retain glossy edge reflections. This is an artistic inference from tinted acrylic, not a certified optical value or fitment claim.

Rendering priority: distinguish smooth dielectric gloss paint, satin coated frame and exhaust, finely rough cast engine, soft vinyl saddle and rough rubber. Reserve the strongest metallic response for bare silver fork tubes, fasteners, discs, tank cap and exhaust cap. Any numerical shader values are artistic approximations to these images.
