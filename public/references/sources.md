# Rebel 500 visual references

Collected 2026-09-30 for the local 3D reconstruction. All photos remain Honda's copyrighted reference material; these are reference photos, not an official Honda CAD model. The baseline is the 2025 standard Rebel 500 shown on Honda Taiwan. Japan-market photos supplement obscured angles; regional equipment can differ.

Taiwan identifies the standard Rebel 500 separately from 500S. The S equipment includes the headlight cowl, fork covers/gaiters, diamond seat and bronze wheels. Do not silently include those parts on the standard baseline. The primary proportions are a low 690 mm seat, 1490 mm wheelbase, 130/90-16 front and 150/80-16 rear tires. Dimensions are reference constraints rather than an assertion of CAD accuracy.

## Material hierarchy inferred from photographs

Use distinct finishes rather than one universal black. Painted tank/fenders have broad smooth clear-coat reflections; tubular frame is black coated metal; cast engine cases are charcoal with fine roughness; radiator plastic and airbox panels are less reflective; tires are near-black rough rubber; seat is black vinyl with fine grain, soft highlight and stitched perimeter. Exposed silver is limited to selected fasteners, fork tubes, cap, cam plugs, brake discs and exhaust cap. Avoid silver wheel spoke faces: the observed rims/spokes are black with specular edge reflections.

## Limits

Photographs cannot reveal hidden geometry or certify accessory mount compatibility. Some official studio imagery may itself be CG, as Honda Japan notes. Reconstruction should be described as photo-referenced rather than a factory CAD or scan. Preserve the actual interactive 3D geometry and accessory controls; photo references supplement the model and are not a substitute for it.
